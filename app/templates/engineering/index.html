Engineering notes

  • Gain is dimensionless when numerator and denominator use matching units.
  • ADC step size depends on the stated endpoint/code convention; this tool uses Vref / 2^N.
  • RC time constant uses resistance in ohms and capacitance in farads.
  • Unsigned N-bit values range from zero through 2^N − 1.
